# Pattern — Search (candidate)

## Purpose
Search is the primary discovery entry, not a dashboard widget.

## Results
Group or label results by entity type without forcing separate searches. Every row/card must expose enough identity/disambiguation to avoid name-only ambiguity.

## Narrow
Single-column result list; type, primary name and minimal disambiguation visible without horizontal scrolling.

## Wide
Results may use denser rows or restrained cards, but preserve reading order and keyboard navigation. Filters are secondary to query/results and must collapse cleanly.

## Required states
Empty query, no results, partial/error state, long names, mixed scripts, keyboard focus and loading.
